wikiPhidia privacy

This covers the wikiPhidia app and its browser extension. Neither has a server or an account of its own, and neither collects, sells or shares anything about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your lessons, words, meanings, notes and study progress are kept in your own browser.
  • Words you pick up with the extension wait in the extension's own browser storage until the wikiPhidia app, open in the same browser, takes them in.
  • Dictionary results are cached in the browser so the same word is not looked up twice.

What is sent out, and only when you use it

  • The word or sentence you look up or translate is sent to the dictionary or translation service that answers it (such as Google Translate, MyMemory, Cambridge, Oxford, Longman, Collins, Merriam-Webster, Vocabulary.com, Free Dictionary, Wiktionary, Youdao, MDBG, Jisho, krdict), the same as visiting their pages yourself.
  • The extension reads the page you are on only to mark words you already have and to find the sentence around a word you pick; that text is not sent anywhere else.
  • The extension checks the app's public page for word levels and for its own latest version.
